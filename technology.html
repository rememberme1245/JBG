JBG R&D Architecture

Engineering Without Excess

Inside every proposed JBG system is a disciplined thermal, electrical, and mechanical architecture engineered around sustained real-world workloads rather than short burst marketing numbers.

3.1 · Cooling

Liquid & External Cooling

Micro-skived CPU cold plates inside laptops pair with an optional tempered-glass External Water Cooler for JBG Play, unlocking 5.8–6.1 GHz CPU speeds and GPU overclocking.

3.2 · Thermals

Thermal Architecture

Side-mounted micro-fin radiators and tuned laminar airflow channels sustain 25–35 W (Air) and 45–54 W (Pro) targets with restrained acoustic output.

3.3 · Mobility

Portable Computing

CNC-machined aluminium-magnesium and carbon-composite structures deliver torsional rigidity across laptops, handhelds, and 0.7-inch displays.

3.4 · Efficiency

Power Management

Clear separation between peak single-core boost and sustained all-core envelopes ensures honest battery and adapter sizing across workloads.

3.5 · Validation

Engineering Innovation

Every specification remains labelled proposed until validated across repeatable thermal soak, acoustic chamber, and application benchmarks.

3.6 · Craft

Design Philosophy

Quiet luxury surfaces — off-white, matte charcoal, deep black, and anodized titanium — free of visual clutter or artificial RGB distractions.
